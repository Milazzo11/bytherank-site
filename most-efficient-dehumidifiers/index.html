How this ranking is scored

We took the 10 portable ENERGY STAR dehumidifiers with the highest certified Integrated Energy Factor (IEF) and scored them on efficiency, capacity and yearly energy use.

  • Efficiency (IEF) (55%): liters of water removed per kWh; higher means a lower power bill for the same dryness (higher is better)
  • Capacity (30%): certified water removal per day under the DOE test (higher is better)
  • Annual energy use (15%): the certified yearly electricity use (lower is better)

Each number is scored against the best on this page (best = 100%; e.g. 90% of the best capacity = 0.9) and multiplied by its weight; the total is out of 100. Full method.

FAQ

What is IEF?

Integrated Energy Factor: liters of water removed per kilowatt-hour, including standby power, measured under the U.S. DOE test procedure. Higher is better.

Is a bigger dehumidifier less efficient?

Not necessarily. On this list capacity ranges from 32 to 49.86 pints per day; compare IEF directly.

Where do the numbers come from?

From the U.S. EPA ENERGY STAR certified dehumidifier dataset. We rank the certified results; we did not run our own tests.
