How this ranking is scored

We compared the 10 zero-ozone ENERGY STAR air purifiers with the highest certified pollen CADR, then weighed pollen, dust and efficiency.

  • Pollen CADR (45%): how fast it removes pollen, the largest test particle (higher is better)
  • Dust CADR (35%): how fast it removes dust (higher is better)
  • Smoke CADR per watt (20%): clean air per watt of electricity (higher is better)

Each number is scored against the best on this page (best = 100%; e.g. 90% of the best capacity = 0.9) and multiplied by its weight; the total is out of 100. Full method.

FAQ

Will an air purifier stop my allergies?

It lowers airborne pollen and dust in the room it runs in; it does not remove allergens from bedding or carpets. Ask a doctor about treatment.

What is the difference between pollen and dust CADR?

They are separate certified tests with different particle sizes. On this page pollen CADR ranges from 450 to 450 cfm and dust CADR from 378 to 586 cfm.

Do any of these produce ozone?

No. Every model on this page has 0.00 ppb certified ozone emissions.

Where do these numbers come from?

From the U.S. EPA ENERGY STAR certified Room Air Cleaners dataset (retrieved 2026-11-01). We rank the certified CADR and energy results; we did not test the units ourselves.
