How this ranking is scored

We compared the 10 zero-ozone ENERGY STAR air purifiers with the highest certified smoke CADR per watt.

  • Smoke CADR per watt (50%): clean air per watt of electricity (higher is better)
  • Smoke CADR (30%): how fast it removes the smallest particles (smoke) (higher is better)
  • Annual energy use (20%): certified yearly electricity use (lower is better)

Each number is scored against the best on this page (best = 100%; e.g. 90% of the best capacity = 0.9) and multiplied by its weight; the total is out of 100. Full method.

FAQ

What does CADR per watt mean?

Clean air delivery rate (cubic feet per minute of filtered air) divided by the power the unit draws. It is the efficiency figure ENERGY STAR certifies.

How much does it cost to run one of these?

The certified yearly use on this page ranges from 32 to 335 kWh. Multiply by your electricity rate (for example 0.17 dollars per kWh) for the yearly cost.

Do any of these produce ozone?

No. Every model on this page has 0.00 ppb certified ozone emissions.

Where do these numbers come from?

From the U.S. EPA ENERGY STAR certified Room Air Cleaners dataset (retrieved 2026-11-01). We rank the certified CADR and energy results; we did not test the units ourselves.
