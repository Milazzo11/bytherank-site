How this ranking is scored

We compared the 10 zero-ozone ENERGY STAR certified air purifiers with the highest certified smoke CADR. Smoke CADR carries most of the weight; the rest goes to the five-year electricity cost, computed from the certified yearly energy use and the current EIA U.S. average residential price. Rated room size is left out of the score because ENERGY STAR derives it from smoke CADR.

  • Smoke CADR (70%): how fast the unit removes smoke-sized particles, the smallest of the three certified test particles (higher is better)
  • 5-year electricity cost (30%): the certified yearly energy use priced at the current U.S. average residential electricity rate (EIA) over five years: the running cost you will actually pay (lower is better) Computed: certified kWh per year x EIA U.S. average residential price x 5.

Each number is scored against the best on this page (best = 100%; e.g. 90% of the best capacity = 0.9) and multiplied by its weight; the total is out of 100. Full method.

FAQ

How much smoke CADR do the models here have?

From 413 to 577 cfm, the highest values among 204 zero-ozone certified models.

What does it cost to run one of these?

At the U.S. average residential electricity price of 0.1816 dollars per kWh (EIA, 2026-08), the certified yearly energy use of the models on this page costs 213 to 445 US dollars over five years. Your state's rate may be higher or lower.

Why only zero-ozone models?

Ozone irritates the lungs (U.S. EPA). Every model on this page has zero certified ozone emissions in the ENERGY STAR data.

Where do these numbers come from?

CADR, room size and energy use come from the U.S. EPA ENERGY STAR certified Room Air Cleaners dataset (retrieved 2026-11-01); the electricity price comes from the U.S. EIA Electric Power Monthly (2026-08). We did not test the units ourselves; we rank the certified results and compute the running cost.

Is noise included?

No. The certification data does not include noise, so check the maker's decibel rating if the unit will run in a bedroom.
