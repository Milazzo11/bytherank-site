How this ranking is scored

We took zero-ozone ENERGY STAR air purifiers certified for rooms of 500 sq ft or more and compared the 10 with the highest smoke CADR.

  • Smoke CADR (40%): how fast it removes the smallest particles (smoke) (higher is better)
  • Dust CADR (20%): how fast it removes dust (higher is better)
  • Smoke CADR per watt (25%): clean air per watt of electricity (higher is better)
  • Rated room size (15%): the largest room it is certified to clean (higher is better)

Each number is scored against the best on this page (best = 100%; e.g. 90% of the best capacity = 0.9) and multiplied by its weight; the total is out of 100. Full method.

FAQ

Is one big purifier better than two small ones?

For one open space a single high-CADR unit is simpler; for separate rooms, smaller units per room work better because walls block airflow.

Do any of these produce ozone?

No. Every model on this page has 0.00 ppb certified ozone emissions.

Where do these numbers come from?

From the U.S. EPA ENERGY STAR certified Room Air Cleaners dataset (retrieved 2026-11-01). We rank the certified CADR and energy results; we did not test the units ourselves.
