How this ranking is scored

We compared the 10 ENERGY STAR whole-home (ducted) dehumidifiers with the highest certified capacity.

  • Capacity (45%): certified water removal per day (higher is better)
  • Efficiency (IEF) (45%): liters removed per kWh: lower power bills (higher is better)
  • Annual energy use (10%): certified yearly electricity use (lower is better)

Each number is scored against the best on this page (best = 100%; e.g. 90% of the best capacity = 0.9) and multiplied by its weight; the total is out of 100. Full method.

FAQ

Do I need a whole-house unit or a portable one?

Whole-house units are installed in the HVAC system and need a professional; portables plug in and serve one area. For one damp basement, a portable is usually enough.

How efficient are they?

Certified IEF on this page ranges from 2.22 to 3.26 liters per kWh, higher than most portables.

Where do these numbers come from?

From the U.S. EPA ENERGY STAR certified Dehumidifiers dataset (retrieved 2026-11-01). We rank the certified results; we did not run our own tests.
